# A Protocol for a Multicenter Trial of a Digital Cognitive Training Programme in Older Adults

N. Author, F. Author — Institute of Gerontology, Example University.
Corresponding author: n.author@example.edu

## Abstract

This study protocol describes a two-arm randomized controlled trial of a
digital cognitive training programme versus an active control in
community-dwelling older adults. The trial protocol was approved by the
regional ethics committee and the study is registered. Recruitment
began in March 2025.

## Introduction

Age-related cognitive decline motivates scalable interventions [1] [2].
Computerized training trials show mixed results [3]. We describe the
design of a pragmatic trial.

## Methods: trial design

Participants aged 65-80 with subjective memory complaints will be
randomly assigned 1:1 to 12 weeks of adaptive cognitive training or an
active control (general health education). The primary outcome is a
composite memory score at 12 months. The full eligibility criteria and
the complete list of primary and secondary outcomes are published on the
trial registry. Sample size was calculated for 80% power.

## Ethics and dissemination

The study protocol was approved by the regional ethics committee
(REF-2024-887). Written informed consent will be obtained. Results will
be disseminated regardless of direction of effect.

## Discussion

This protocol paper presents the rationale and design of a trial
addressing limitations of prior cognitive-training studies. Strengths
include the active comparator and long follow-up.

## Limitations

As a protocol, no results are reported; the design may require amendment
after recruitment experience.
